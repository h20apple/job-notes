Privacy Policy for FiberNotes

Effective date: [DATE]

FiberNotes is a job notes app designed to help users create and save job-related notes on their device.

Information We Collect

FiberNotes does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app may allow you to enter job numbers, job notes, light readings, job type information, garden type, fibre length, and other job-related details. This information is stored locally on your device only.

We do not operate servers for FiberNotes, and we do not receive the information you enter into the app.

Data Storage

Any notes or job information you enter are stored locally on your device using Apple’s local app storage.

If you delete the app, the locally stored app data may also be removed from your device.

Third-Party Services

FiberNotes does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking tools, or external data collection services.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone.

Children’s Privacy

FiberNotes is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Choices

You can remove saved notes inside the app using the clear/delete option. You can also delete the app from your device to remove locally stored app data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
